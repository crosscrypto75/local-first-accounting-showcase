# Synthetic Invoice Flow

> **SYNTHETIC EXAMPLE — NOT A PRIVATE SCHEMA, RECORD, OR ACCOUNTING RULE**

```text
1. A user supplies a document.
2. The application creates a previewable candidate record.
3. Deterministic checks validate required information and consistency.
4. The candidate remains in validation if a category or match is unconfirmed.
5. A human confirms or corrects the result.
6. The accepted operation remains linked to its supporting document.
```

No real supplier, amount, date, identifier, tax treatment, or accounting category is used in this example.
