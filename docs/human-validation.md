# Human Validation

Imported and manually entered operations can remain “to validate” until their category is confirmed. This gives uncertainty a visible state instead of forcing an early decision.

## Why validation matters

- A document may be incomplete or ambiguous.
- An import may contain duplicates or unexpected values.
- A likely category can still be wrong in business context.
- A consequential action needs accountable approval.

Human review can confirm, correct, defer, or reject a candidate. Supporting documents remain attached to operations so the basis for a decision stays available later.

Confidence is not authority. Even a high-confidence automated suggestion should not bypass the validation boundary when the accounting consequence requires confirmation.
